## 1. Conclusion — one plain-English sentence per conjunct

The conclusion is a 12-way conjunction (`Ziel.lean:2394-2416`):

1. (`:2394`) The machine run is disciplined: `Gesittet M.lauf` holds, i.e. all
   five run well-formedness conditions W1–W5 (`Wettlauf.lean:181-195`).
2. (`:2395-2396`) Every per-thread projection of the run is consistent
   (`Konsistent`, `Satz.lean:243`) and every observed event is good (`e.gut`).
3. (`:2397-2399`) At the last world of the joint chain, every member thread's
   contract conjunction (`SpecQ` of `QRequires`/`QEnsures`,
   `InterferenzAllgemein.lean:1069`) holds.
4. (`:2400-2401`) The probe catches the named deadline: some tick lies between
   the deadline and the use, and the deadline outcome answers the named
   hardware assumption `fortschritt` (`Fristlauf.lean:142-144`).
5. (`:2402`) The lowering witness keeps its cap: `hLowering.proPrimitiv ≤ 18`
   (`Ziel.lean:83-86`).
6. (`:2403-2405`) The sequential outcome is classified: it is control flow
   (`ok`, `zurueck`, `grund`, `leave`, `next`), the author's own logic
   (`logik`), or a named hardware assumption (`hardware`) — nothing else
   (`zwei_fehler`, `Satz.lean:50`; `Logik`/`Hardware` at
   `Semantik.lean:275`/`287`).
7. (`:2406`) Marks are single-threaded: the code projection of the run never
   names one mark in two threads (`Marken.Einfaedig` over `laufProj`).
8. (`:2407-2412`) Unshared carriers are single-threaded: two accesses touching
   the same unshared carrier (table with `geteilt = false`, global with
   `ggeteilt = false`) belong to the same thread.
9. (`:2413`) The world history has one world per step plus the start world
   (`M.welten.length = M.tiefe + 1`).
10. (`:2414`) Every event recorded in every world history entry is good.
11. (`:2415`) The last world of the history is a live thread world
    (some thread's memory read over its own trace, `M.speicher.welt`).
12. (`:2416`) The two named conflicting accesses are happens-before ordered,
    one way or the other (`HB`, `Wettlauf.lean:198-203`).

## 3. Applicability flags — premises that exclude ordinary programs

1. `hGuardEx`: every carrier must have a guard lock. Ordinary lock-free or
   unguarded shared data has none; the theorem cannot speak about it.
2. `hWatch` for globals: the table discharge `wache_aus_schuld` is
   table-only (U003 is table-only, `Syntax.lean:181-182` per the prose at
   `InterferenzAllgemein.lean:1476-1480`). Programs sharing globals owe an
   undischarged checker-side watch.
3. `hDeck` (`GeteiltGedeckt`): any two threads writing one carrier must show
   a shared flag plus a common lock. Ordinary racy or lock-free programs are
   outside the theorem, not inside a failed obligation.
4. `hForm` (`InvariantForm`): every thread's contract conjunction must
   coincide with some carrier invariant at every world. Ordinary functional
   contracts (e.g. "result equals f(args)") are not resource invariants; the
   non-invariant fragment still owes the per-run `InterferenceFree` check
   exactly as booked (`InterferenzAllgemein.lean:1181-1183`), which this
   theorem does not take.
5. `hBlattAll`: uniform two-way preservation of both contract sides over
   every leaf firing at every reachable machine. Ordinary contracts that a
   thread's own step may legitimately break and re-establish (e.g. a
   counter invariant mid-update) do not satisfy the per-firing `↔`; only
   memory-constant contracts discharge, via
   `hBlattAll_speicherfest_aus_feuerung` (`Maschine.lean:4093`).
6. `hSeedAll` (ensures half): `QEnsures` must already hold at the start
   world. For ordinary contracts the postcondition is established by
   running, not assumed at entry.
7. `hJw`/`hJsf`: the joint chain `J` must be supplied and equated with the
   machine run. Ordinary users have no `J`; the only discharger,
   `kette_aus_lauf_bezeugt_closed` (`Ziel.lean:2235`), narrows to
   single-thread runs, while this goal stays two-thread (conclusion
   conjunct 12 needs `g₁ ≠ g₂`).
8. `hMSep`: two threads running the same function share its marks in their
   extracted texts (`progAus` flattens per thread, `Extraktion.lean:2572`),
   which violates mark separation — the most ordinary multithreaded shape
   (same code on two threads) does not reach the W4 discharge.
9. Probe premises (`hpd`/`hdl`/`hspace` + `S c hstart`): the theorem speaks
   of exactly one deadline pair, and `hspace` (the use waits out the whole
   sampling window) is owed per use as a named hardware assumption. Ordinary
   programs have many deadlines; 28 of 29 deadlines have no running probe
   (booked remainder at `Ziel.lean:434-441`).
10. `hO` (`GutO`): oracles that emit events of their own are excluded
    (the `spur`-preservation conjunct at `Satz.lean:796`; remainder booked
    at `Ziel.lean:1106-1111`). Ordinary device oracles that log on firing
    do not satisfy it.
11. `hLowering`: the cap leg covers the numeric bound only; per-primitive
    count preservation for the seven modeled ops stands downstream
    (`Budget.lean:681-690`, cited not imported), and 30 un-decided C forms
    in real output are outside it (prose at `Ziel.lean:68-73`).
12. Conclusion conjunct 12 narrows the order claim to one named table
    carrier and two named accesses (`t₀`, `hw₁`, `hw₂`): whatever the rest
    of an ordinary program races on is not ordered by this theorem. Shared
    globals, more than two conflicting sections, and restoring writers stay
    open (remainder at `Ziel.lean:908-917`).
