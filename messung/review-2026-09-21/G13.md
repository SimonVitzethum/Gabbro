## The four questions of the brief

**1. Is the ATS/SPARK doc accurate about Gabbro?** Not before `7056e6b4`. The doc never says
"Gabbro is verified", but its Gabbro column described the architecture in the present tense:

- "Lean checks the certificate" per program;
- "the per-program certificate is re-checked in Lean";
- "the answer is yes with a certificate";
- templates "machine-checked in Isabelle/Lean";
- data-race freedom "proved".

Measured against README §4–§6 and TODO §0d, the state is different:

- the chain is closed for three programs (104, 108 and 124);
- 5 of 21 templates are bound to the real semantics;
- everywhere else the Rust checker, the exporter and the emitter are trusted, and the doc's
  trust list left them out.

Other errors in the doc:

- The time row ignored that declared `costs` are not in `Deklaration` and that the exporter
  drops `deadline`.
- §5 did not name what `Ziel` leaves out: pool duplicates, same-core preemption, and DMA, which
  has no leg in `Zielsatz/`.
- §8 listed "probabilistic statements, dynamic unbounded structures" as items of `Spec.lean`'s
  NOT-CLAIMED list. They are not in it. The doc copied this from AGENTS §2, which carried the
  same error (fixed in `4dfd9985`).

All of this is corrected. A status paragraph now says that the Gabbro column is architecture.
The ATS and SPARK columns were not changed. They were read for plausibility only, not
researched.

**2. AGENTS §7.** Stale since 2026-09-17: it said N391 / 1052 / 147 / 221. Measured on master:

reservations against what was actually taken:

The rule for reserves that were never used is now written down (`bc81e534`): they stay with the
follow-up work of their wall. For example, N411–415 is the block for G07's exhaustiveness
refusal.

**3. OFFEN.md.** O17's facts check out. The per-core cells are `static _Atomic` with relaxed
load and store (`emit.rs` ~2476), so the worst case is lost updates, not a C data race. The
exporter does refuse `accumulates`. One sentence was false (wave B has no task for this), and
O17 covered only the per-core half. Two absences found by two reviews each are now entries:
