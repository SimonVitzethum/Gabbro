## Open findings, by severity

### 1. (medium) `cform_nested_read_zeuge` is degenerate: its equation is `none = none`

`grammatik/Grammatik/CFormNested.lean`, `nzL := fun _ => none` and the
witness below it. `ev` on `.idx` calls `ptrAdd L q …`
(`CFormen.lean:635-645`), and `ptrAdd` returns `none` when `L q.blk = none`
(`CSpeicher.lean:576-578`). So both `nestedRead …` and `flatRead …` evaluate
to `none` under `nzL`, and the witness proves only that the premises are
jointly satisfiable, not that a nested read ever succeeds and equals the flat
read. The second conjunct (`nv_lauf.1`, a G-side run of `Verschachtelt.lean`)
is unrelated to the C evaluation it is conjoined with -- decorative in the
sense of the lane-147 rejection. Scenario: a regression making every nested
`idx` stuck would leave both the lemma's instance and the witness green.
The lemma itself is sound (it is `ev_idx_nested` lifted through `ld`, which
also holds in the stuck case).
**Repair (not done: cannot be built here, and `rfl` through `natLay`/`bLoad`
is not obviously reducible):** a layout
`fun _ => some ⟨natLay 12 [.int false .w32], .plain, 0⟩` plus a conjunct
that `ev … (flatRead …) nzSt nzRho = some (.int 0, nzSt)`.
TODO.md's lane-205 entry does not claim more than address level, and the
lane report says so plainly; the gap is the witness, not the wording.

### 2. (medium) Lane 206's "checker" does not feed the simulation

`grammatik/Grammatik/SimPruef.lean`, `simpruef_liefert`: the `SimC` package
is `sim124 passes w hw` regardless of the certificate `c`; `c` only
contributes four list equalities to literals. Before `e92c458e` those
literals were not tied to `gOfA`/`heldGA`/`gOfB`/`heldGB` by any theorem
(docstring "read off" only), and the Rust side
(`corrcert.rs`, `SimCert124::erwartet_*`) hard-codes the same literals, so
printer, checker and expectation were three copies of one hand-typed table.
With the fix, the tables are pinned to `R124`'s functions, but the
certificate still carries no information the simulation depends on. The
lane report's own CUTS say the segments stay hand-fed; the merge message
"simpruef_liefert turning the printed certificate into the sim124
conclusion" and TODO.md ("checks the printed certificate into the `sim124`
conclusion") read bigger than that. Scenario: any program #2 needs a
checker whose output depends on the certificate (`SegPasst` per step);
nothing in this lane is reusable for that except the JSON shape.
