## 1. The criterion, stated before the answers

Run `gabbro emit`, not `gabbro pruefe`. Then per refusal, name the pass and ask what it read:

| | verdict | test |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **plumbing** | the pass has **no arm** for a well-formed construct that has a faithful C shape. Repair under `crates/` alone: no source word, no grammar production, no rule weakened. |
| **2** | **a rule decision** | the refusal is correct and Gabbro would have to **stop making it**, or gain a **grammar production**. |
| **3** | **the program is wrong** | the source states a fact that is wrong or missing. No `crates/` change supplies it, and the measured consequence of not refusing is that **`cc` refuses instead**. |

### `M143` (#14) and `M140` (#9, 11, 12, 15, 16, 17) — `cc` says it, not I

> **The objection to answer here, because it is the only one that would make this plumbing:**
> *could the emitter insert a cast and make the C compile?* It could make it **compile**, and

> third. It is also against the emitter's stated policy at the one place it refuses:
> `emit.rs`:2506, *"the emitter refuses by name instead of emitting something plausible — a
> generator that guesses undoes every pass in front of it."* **A generator arm that guesses an
> address is not the missing arm; it is `D20`'s shape with the soundness question answered the
> other way.**

## 5. What follows for `H`

`H = 1` stands, and the reason is stronger than the count.

* The obligation booked against `F03` — *"the generated C computes what the fragment says"* —
  **presupposes that the fragment says something well-formed**, and nine of the eighteen are
  the file saying something that is not. A hanging *plumbing* obligation over a program that
  does not type-check is a category error: there is no arm to write. **That is a statement
  about the measure, not about the file.**

  traverse unconditionally, and its own text says lowering it computes a different program.
  That is «B10», it is open, and `645ddca` is what removing it looks like.
* Five of the eighteen (`N035`) sit at a declaration the fragment never calls through, so
  they could not affect the generated C either way.

**What would have made one of them plumbing**, named so the classification is falsifiable: a
refusal at a construct the checker accepts and the emitter is merely silent about, with a
faithful C shape available — `D21` and `D22`'s shape exactly. `F03` has one emitter refusal,
`C001` at `:185`, and it is refusal **by name and on purpose**.
