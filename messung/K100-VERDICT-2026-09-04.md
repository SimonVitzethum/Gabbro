## 5. Does the conjunction entail *100 % plumbing coverage*?

**No.** Reasoned fresh from today's code, not repeated from the audit's prose:

1. **No code computes a percentage anywhere.** `H` is `10 - |pierced|` (§1); nothing divides
   it by `dokumente/PFLICHTEN.md`'s obligation count (`239 = 173 K + 66 L`, itself read out of
   a hand-typed third column — `zaehle-pflichten.py --spalten` counts that column, it does not
   judge it). "100 %" and "173/173" are sentences in `PLAN.md` and `PFLICHTEN.md`, not return
   values. `PLAN.md:1248` is itself a live instance of the drift this whole exercise is about:
   it carries *"173 = 168 getragen + 5 hängend"* as "der heutige Stand," in the very section
   that introduces `K100` — and `--spalten`/`--haengend` today give `173 K + 0 L` and `H = 1`,
   not `168`/`5`. The paragraph asserting the headline's own arithmetic is stale.
2. **The denominator `H` runs over is ten fragments, fixed, chosen for difficulty**, and
   incapable of growing: `zaehle-pflichten.py`'s own closing text says a `H = 0` over them
   "bliebe Falle 80, solange kein Korpus daneben steht" — and §4 shows that corpus does not
   exist independently of the rules it is measured against.
3. **`L`'s job in the table is to price the cheapest way to fake `H`** — moving an obligation
   from the checker side into unproved generator trust instead of discharging it. §2 shows
   that price is currently zero: no assertion in the tree can refuse a `Getragen` relabelling,
   only report the new total. The gate meant to prevent gaming `H` cannot currently object to
   the exact move it exists to price.
4. **`A_p` is a real, healthy measurement of a different axis** (hardware-assumption
   falsifiability) that happens to share a table with three plumbing-coverage proxies. Its
   soundness does not transfer to theirs.

**What would make the conjunction entail the headline:** (a) a coded ratio — obligations
discharged over obligations named, both read from the same mechanically-derived source, not
a hand-typed document column; (b) an `H` that classifies by running `gabbro emit` and reading
*why* it refuses, over a denominator that is the actual `PFLICHTEN.md` obligation set rather
than ten curated fragments — or, short of that, an explicit statement that `H` is a fragment
countdown and not a coverage measure; (c) an executable `L <= N` and a rule (not a comment)
that a `Getragen` relabelling without matching proof progress is itself a finding; (d) a
second corpus assembled, and its rules extended, by a party or a process that did not also
write the fragments — or, short of that, dropping the `K2` row until one exists; and a
denominator for `A_p` sourced from `git ls-files` rather than a live filesystem walk. None of
these four exist today, and the tree already says so, in different words, in three places.
